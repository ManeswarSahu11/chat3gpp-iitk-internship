Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document defines User Equipment (UE) policies that are used to configure the UE for Proximity-based Services (ProSe) in 5G System (5GS) based on the architectural requirements defined in 3GPP TS 23.304 . The protocol aspects for 5G ProSe are described in 3GPP TS 24.554 .

Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > Overview
Content: The ProSe policy in 5GS includes: [ a) ] UE policies for 5G ProSe direct discovery (see clause 4.2); UE policies for 5G ProSe direct communications (see clause 4.3); UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-network relay (see clause 4.4); UE policies for 5G ProSe usage information reporting (see clause 4.5); and UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-UE relay (see clause 4.6). The ProSe policy can be delivered from the PCF to the UE. The UE policy delivery procedure is specified in 3GPP TS 24.501 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > UE policies for 5G ProSe direct discovery
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe direct discovery are defined in clause 5.2.3 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe direct discovery is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > UE policies for 5G ProSe direct communications
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe direct communications are defined in clause 5.2.4 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe direct communications is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-network relay
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-network relay UE are defined in clause 5.2.5 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-network relay is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 . The UE policies for 5G ProSe remote UE are defined in clause 5.2.5 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe remote UE is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > UE policies for 5G ProSe usage information reporting
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe usage information reporting are defined in clause 5.2.6 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe usage information reporting is specified in 3GPP TS 32.277 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for 5G ProSe > UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-UE relay
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-UE relay UE are defined in clause 5.2.7 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe UE-to-UE relay UE is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 . The UE policies for 5G ProSe end UE are defined in clause 5.2.7 of 3GPP TS 24.554 . The generic description of the UE policies for 5G ProSe end UE is specified in 3GPP TS 23.304 .
Title Path: Encoding of UE policies for 5G ProSe > Overview
Content: The UE policies for 5G ProSe are provided to the UE in a 5G ProSe policy (ProSeP) UE policy part using the UE policy delivery service as specified in 3GPP TS 24.501 annex D.
