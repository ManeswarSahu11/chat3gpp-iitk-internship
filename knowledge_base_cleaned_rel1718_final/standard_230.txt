Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document specifies the standards allowed to implement Layer 1 on the Iupc interface. The specification of transmission delay requirements and O\&M requirements is not in the scope of the present document. In the following "Layer 1" and "Physical Layer" are assumed to be synonymous.
Title Path: Definitions, symbols and abbreviations
Content: For the purposes of the present document, the terms and definitions given in 3GPP TS 25.411 apply.
Title Path: Iupc Layer 1
Content: The Iupc Layer 1 shall comply with the requirements of clause 4 in TS 25.411 .
