Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document specifies the Solution Sets for the IRP whose semantics is specified in Notification Log (NL) IRP IS (3GPP TS 32.332 ). This Solution Set specification is related to 3GPP TS 32.332 V14.0.X.

Content: For the purposes of the present document, the terms and definitions given in TR 21.905 , 3GPP TS 32.101 , 3GPP TS 32.102 , 3GPP TS 32.150 , 3GPP TS 32.331 and 3GPP TS 32.332 apply. A term defined in the present document takes precedence over the definition of the same term, if any, in TR 21.905 . notification category: it refers to the set of notifications of one 3GPP IRP Information Service specification A Notification Category is identified by the name of the IRP specification and the IRP specification version number.
