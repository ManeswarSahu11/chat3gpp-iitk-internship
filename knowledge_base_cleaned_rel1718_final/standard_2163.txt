Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document is a technical report for Dual Connectivity of 2 bands LTE inter-band CA (2DL/1UL) and 1 NR band (1DL/1UL) under Rel-17 time frame. The purpose is to gather the relevant background information and studies in order to address Dual connectivity (DC) band combinations of 3 different bands DL with 2 different bands UL (2 different LTE bands and 1 NR band) for the Rel-17 band combinations. The actual requirements are added to the corresponding technical specification.
