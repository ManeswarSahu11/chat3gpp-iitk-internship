Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document defines User Equipment (UE) policies that are used to configure the UE for Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) services in 5G System (5GS) based on the architectural requirements defined in 3GPP TS 23.287 . The protocol aspects for V2X services in 5G System (5GS) are described in 3GPP TS 24.587 .

Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for V2X > Overview
Content: The V2XP in 5GS include: UE policies for V2X communication over PC5 (see clause 4.2); and UE policies for V2X communication over Uu (see clause 4.3). The V2XP can be delivered from the PCF to the UE. The UE policy delivery procedure is specified in 3GPP TS 24.501 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for V2X > UE policies for V2X communication over PC5
Content: The UE policies for V2X communication over PC5 are defined in clause 5.2.3 of 3GPP TS 24.587 . NOTE: The generic description of the UE policies for V2X communication over PC5 are specified in 3GPP TS 23.287 .
Title Path: Descriptions of UE policies for V2X > UE policies for V2X communication over Uu
Content: The UE policies for V2X communication over Uu are defined in clause 5.2.4 of 3GPP TS 24.587 . NOTE: The generic description of the UE policies for V2X communication over Uu are specified in 3GPP TS 23.287 .
Title Path: Encoding of UE policies for V2X > Overview
Content: The UE policies for V2X are provided to the UE in a V2X policy (V2XP) UE policy part using the UE policy delivery service as specified in 3GPP TS 24.501 annex D.
