Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document contains objectives, requirements and test cases that are specific to the MnF network product class. It refers to the Catalogue of General Security Assurance Requirements and formulates specific adaptions of the requirements and test cases given there, as well as specifying requirements and test cases unique to the MnF network product class. In the present document, the MnF network product class represents independently deployed management product supporting 3GPP defined management services.
