Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document describes the Information Model definition for Fault Management, Configuration Management and Performance measurements of Home eNodeBs. The stage 2 definitions captured in the present document shall be met via type 1 interface between HeNB and HMS.
Title Path: Definitions and abbreviations > Definitions
Content: For the purposes of the present document, the terms and definitions given in TR 21.905 and the following apply. A term defined in the present document takes precedence over the definition of the same term, if any, in TR 21.905. 3G Home NodeB and Home eNodeB: These terms, their derivations and abbreviations are used synonymously throughout the present document.
