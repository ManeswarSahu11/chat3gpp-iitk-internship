Title Path: Background
Content: At 3GPP RAN4\#91-e meeting, a basket Work Item on ``Power class 2 UE for NR inter-band CA and SUL configurations with x (x 2) bands DL and y (y=1, 2) bands UL'' was approved for Rel-17, of which the objectives are as follows: Specify the band-combination specific RF requirements for all listed power class 2 NR inter-band UL CA and SUL configurations with power class 2 on NUL and power class 3 on SUL (xDL/yUL, x 2, y=1, 2). Analysing combinations that have self-desensitization, applicable $ $T and $ $R and reference sensitivity exceptions including MSD test cases. Other additional impact to the requirements due to the high power on UL, if necessary. The present document is a technical report for this basket Work Item, and aims to study the band-combination specific requirements for each of combinations captured in table 1-1.
