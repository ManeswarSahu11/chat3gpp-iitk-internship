Title Path: Concepts and background
Content: The architecture for management of WLAN AP is conformant to the management reference model as defined in Figure 1 of TS 32.101 where the WLAN AP is depicted as NE. The system context of the WLAN management is in compliance with the System Context A, defined in Figure 4.7.1 in TS 32.150 . Based on the above characteristics, the present document defines respective WLAN management NRM IRP requirements.
