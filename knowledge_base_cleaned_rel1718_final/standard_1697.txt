Title Path: Concepts and background > Overview
Content: The architecture for management of WLAN AP is conformant to the Management reference model as defined in Figure 1 of TS 32.101 where the WLAN AP is depicted as NE. The system context of the WLAN management is in compliance with the System Context A, defined in Figure 4.7.1 in TS 32.150 .
Title Path: Concepts and background > Mapping Function
Content: Figure 4.2-1 provides an example of 3GPP WLAN mapping function. The mapping function is to map the relevant management data produced by WLAN AP in a form suitable for distribution via the Type-2 interface to IRPManager(s). The mapping function is a logical function. Its location, as well as its internal and external interfaces, if any, are out the scope of 3GPP specification.
