Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document is a technical report for Dual Connectivity (DC) of 5 LTE bands (5DL/1UL) and 1 NR band (1DL/1UL) under Rel-17 time frame. The purpose is to gather the relevant background information and studies in order to address Dual Connectivity (DC) of 5 LTE band (5DL/1UL) and 1 NR band (1DL/1UL) for the Rel-17 band combinations. The co-existence analysis and RF front end requirements such as $ $R and $ $T are described based on the band combination basis since such information have no difference between the DC configurations consisting with the same E-UTRA band and the same NR band. The actual requirements are added to the corresponding technical specification.
