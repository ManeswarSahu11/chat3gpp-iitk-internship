Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document specifies the standards allowed to implement Layer 1 on the W1 interface. The W1 interface provides means for interconnecting an ng-eNB-CU and an ng-eNB-DU of an ng-eNB within NG-RAN. The specification of transmission delay requirements and O\&M requirements are not in the scope of the present document. In the following 'Layer 1' and 'Physical Layer' are assumed to be synonymous.
