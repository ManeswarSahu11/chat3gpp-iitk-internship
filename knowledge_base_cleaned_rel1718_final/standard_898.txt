Title Path: Scope
Content: The present document is a technical report for NR sidelink evolution services in Rel-18. The purpose is to specify radio solutions that are necessary for NR to support sidelink services working on unlicensed spectrums.
Title Path: Definitions of terms, symbols and abbreviations > Terms
Content: For the purposes of the present document, the terms given in 3GPP TR 21.905 and the following apply. A term defined in the present document takes precedence over the definition of the same term, if any, in 3GPP TR 21.905 . Con-current operation: The simultaneous transmission and reception of sidelink and Uu interfaces, irrespective of TDM mode and FDM mode, while operation is agnostic of the service used on each interface. Inter-band con-current operation: Operation of NR Uu carrier and NR Sidelink carrier in different operating bands. NR SL CA operation: Aggregation of two or more NR Sidelink component carriers in order to support wider transmission bandwidths. Intra-band SL CA UE: UE that supports NR SL CA operation in a single band NR SL inter-band con-current operating Band：Band combinations of NR Uu carrier and NR Sidelink carrier in different operating bands. NR SL-U UE: UE that supports NR Sidelink operation in unlicensed bands (e.g. n46, n96, n102). PC5: The interface for sidelink transmission. PC3/PC5 UE: UE that supports Power class 3 or Power class 5 UE transmission bandwidth configuration: Set of resource blocks located within the UE channel bandwidth which may be used for transmitting or receiving by the UE. Wideband operation: For a UE that supports shared spectrum channel access, wideband operation refers to operation within a channel larger than 20 MHz in which intra-cell guard bands may be configured to distinguish individual RB-sets

Title Path: Operating bands and channel arrangement for SL evolution > Operating bands > Operating bands for single carrier operation in unlicensed band
Content: NR Sidelink is designed to operate in the unlicensed operating bands in FR1 defined in Table 5.1.1-1. During the NR SL-U development RAN4 identified at least following regional and country specific restrictions for direct connection between the devices within the targeted un-licensed bands (n46, n96 and n102). Client devices are prohibited from connecting directly to another client device in the frequency range 5850 5895 MHz and 5925 7125 MHz in USA, and in the frequency range 5925 7125 MHz in Canada per regulatory rules. Direct connection between client devices in the shared spectrum band is subject to region-specific regulatory rules. For band n96 in USA, SL-U operation is excluded for vehicular UEs.

The mapping depends on the total number of RBs that are allocated in the channel and applies to both Tx and Rx for SL. The mapping must apply to at least one numerology supported by the UE.

Title Path: Transmitter characteristics for NR SL evolution > Tx requirements for NR SL single carrier operation in unlicensed band > Maximum output power for NR SL-U
Content: The SL-U UE Power Classes define the maximum output power for any transmission bandwidth within the channel bandwidth in unlicensed bands. The period of measurement shall be at least one sub frame (1ms). The SL-U UE shall meet the following additional requirements for maximum mean transmission power density specified in Table 6.2F.1-2 in TS38.101-1 when NS is signalled and when transmission overlaps with any portion of the specified frequency range. In case transmission overlaps multiple frequency ranges, the lowest power density requirement applies.
