Title Path: EPC KPI definitions > Accessibility KPI > EPS Attach Success Rate
Content: [ a) ] EPS attach success rate This KPI describes the ratio of the number of successfully performed EPS attach procedures to the number of attempted EPS attach procedures for EPC network and is used to evaluate accessibility provided by EPS and network performance. This KPI is obtained by successful EPS attach procedures divided by attempted EPS attach procedures. MM.EpsAttachAtt.Type MM.EpsAttachSucc.Type Note: above measurements with subcounter.Type should be defined in 3GPP TS 32.426 . EPC Accessibility Percentage RATIO

Title Path: EPC KPI definitions > Utilization KPI > Mean Active Dedicated EPS Bearer Utilization
Content: [ a) ] Mean active dedicated EPS bearer utilization This KPI describes the ratio of the mean number of active dedicated EPS bearer to the maximum number of active dedicated EPS bearers provided by EPC network, and it is used to evaluate utilization performance of EPC network. This KPI is obtained by the mean number of dedicated EPS bearers in active mode divided by the system capacity.
